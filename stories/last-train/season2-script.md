# LAST TRAIN — SEASON 2 "EASTGATE" (script, ready to produce on request)

Canon: continues from **Ending 4 "The Truth"** (Eli alive and missing, Nora safe, note "Go find her").
Logline: Day 2. The door opens on a poisoned city. Nora is nine stops away. Eli's footprints lead into the dark —
and everyone, including the army, is looking for him.

Central mystery: *Why did a stranger risk everything for you and your sister?*
Answer (revealed in every ending, different way): **a year ago Nora, an ER nurse, saved Eli's life.** He recognized you on
the train from her photos ("She talks about you"). He's been paying it forward ever since — Vale, the 40 kids, you.
Theme line that returns: **"Save anyone you can."**

## ENDING 1 — "The Trade"  (TUNNEL → TRADE)

Brann rows you across; the kid holds the dosimeter like a toy that saves lives. You walk blind the last three stops,
counting steps. Eastgate basement: **Nora** in scrubs — and on a cot behind her, **Eli**, pale, an IV in his arm.
NORA: "He walked in at dawn. He's been asking for you." ELI (whisper): "Told you I'd bring them home." Nora explains: a year
ago he came into her ER after a crash, no pulse, and she didn't give up. **"So he never did either."** Last image: three of them
on the cots, morning light through a basement window. ENDING 1 OF 4 · THE TRADE. "You gave away the one thing keeping you
safe. That's exactly what he would have done."

## ENDING 2 — "The Swim"  (TUNNEL → SWIM)

You swim into black water; the flashlight dies; you go under. A hand grabs your wrist and hauls you onto a ledge — **Eli**,
soaked, shivering. "I was waiting. I knew you'd come the hard way." He's sick (cough, shaking hands) but walks you the last
stops. At Eastgate, Nora sees him and drops a tray: "Eli?" Reveal: she saved him a year ago. He collapses into her arms.
NARRATOR: "He's been saving people all night. Now it's someone's turn to save him." ENDING 2 OF 4 · THE SWIM.

## ENDING 3 — "The Checkpoint"  (SURFACE → TELL THE ARMY)

You say "Eastgate General." The truck roars across the bridge. Soldiers storm the basement — Eli is there, beside Nora.
They cuff him. Nora steps between them: "He saved my patients. He saved the kids in Shelter 9. He saved my family." Ortega hesitates… then Ruth's voice on the radio: "Captain, that man saved forty
children." Too late — orders are orders; the truck drives off with Eli. Through the back window he raises his hand.
NARRATOR: "You told the truth again. This time, it cost him everything." ENDING 3 OF 4 · THE CHECKPOINT. (Dark ending.)

## ENDING 4 — "The Long Walk"  (SURFACE → PROTECT HIM)

"Never seen him." Ortega waves you through on foot. Halfway across the bridge the dosimeter screams RED; your knees go.
Someone lifts your arm over their shoulder — grey hoodie gone, Mags's spare mask on: **Eli**. He was watching the checkpoint.
He carries you the rest of the way while ash falls. You wake in Eastgate's basement; Nora at your side. Eli is gone again;
on your chest, Vale's keycard with a new note: "Save anyone you can. Your turn." NARRATOR: "Somewhere out there, he's still
walking." ENDING 4 OF 4 · THE LONG WALK. Hook: Season 3.
