# TRAPPED IN A SUPERMARKET — "FreshCo" (zombie outbreak, decide-series Season 1)

Trend fit (Nov 2026): animated, second-person, day-by-day zombie outbreak stories in an everyday place, numbered parts,
cliffhanger choices. Our edge: real
consequences, planted clues, a dog, and a signature rule.

**Logline:** You only came in for a frozen pizza. Now you're locked in a 24-hour supermarket with a nervous manager, a
cashier who's braver than she looks, an old man and his dog — and outside, the parking lot is full of people who hunt by sound.
The only thing hiding you is the store's terrible music.

**Signature rule:** They hunt by sound. The PA music masks every noise inside. **RULE 1: KEEP THE MUSIC ON.**
Later: **RULE 2: COLD SLOWS THEM** (and the infection). **RULE 3: NEVER RUN.**

**Tone:** suspense + small jokes (the jingle, the pizza), cartoonish scares, **no gore** (bites shown as dark sleeve stains,
turned faces are grey-green with blank eyes). **Biscuit the dog survives every ending.**

## Planted clues (must be visible / heard where listed)

1. **Walt's hearing aid whistles** near loud speakers (Part 1 joke) → betrays the group in Part 3 (both paths).
2. **Dr. Hale's cooler bag:** "HALEWORTH LABS · KEEP COLD · DO NOT OPEN" (Part 1) → it holds the cure samples (Part 2) →
   decides Endings 1 and 2.
3. **Marco's left sleeve** is always pulled down, and he keeps scratching his wrist (Parts 1–2) → he was bitten before the
   doors locked (reveal Part 3A / Ending 3).
4. **Marco's office radio:** in Part 1 he says "Radio's dead." In Part 2 June hears it through his door → rescue at
   Hollis Stadium, Day 3, 6 AM, "first fifty only."
5. **Turned near the freezers move slowly** (Part 1 background gag: one bumps into the frozen-food door and stalls) → Rule 2.

## Sets

FreshCo interior: long aisles with shelves (cereal colors, cans), checkout lanes, glass front with automatic doors and
the parking lot beyond (flickering lot lights, a delivery van), frozen-food wall with glass doors and frost, walk-in
freezer (steel door, porthole window), manager's office (radio, CCTV, PA microphone), back loading bay (roller door,
generator, diesel cans). Exterior: parking lot at night/day, Hollis Stadium gate with army tents (Part 3A/endings).

## ENDINGS (each ~70 s; ENDING N OF 4 card; Biscuit always lives)

**ENDING 1 "The First Dose"** (3A → TELL THE SOLDIERS): You say it. Soldiers drag Marco aside; he screams that he's still
human. Then the army medic sees your cooler: "Haleworth samples? Where did you get these?" Twist: Marco's two days bitten
without turning is exactly what Dr. Hale's cure needs — a living host. Marco becomes the first person ever given the dose.
His veins clear. NARRATOR: "The man who lied to everyone becomes the reason there's a cure." Final: Biscuit asleep on the
truck seat; a radio in the background: "…cure trials begin…" Hook: "Walt?" — a hearing-aid whistle far away.

**ENDING 2 "Patient Zero"** (3A → KEEP HIS SECRET): Inside the stadium, thousands of people, music from the scoreboard.
Night falls; Marco hides his shaking hands. At 3 AM the scoreboard music cuts out, and Marco stands up in the silent stands.
Panic. You run for the last truck with June and Biscuit, cooler in your arms. Marco, still himself for one more second,
pulls the fire alarm to draw the turned away from your truck. NARRATOR: "Your secret cost the stadium. His last second paid
some of it back." Dark ending. Hook: the cooler's last vial is cracked.

**ENDING 3 "Frozen"** (3B → HIDE IN THE FREEZER): Packed into the walk-in with Walt and Biscuit; June last, pulling the
door. Through the porthole, the turned drift past in slow motion, frost forming on their shoulders. Hours. Your fingers go
numb; Biscuit curls against you to keep you warm. Dawn: knocking — soldiers. Marco is with them: he drove to the stadium and
told the army exactly where you were and what the cooler holds. Twist: he locked the door so the van engine would draw the
horde away from you. MARCO: "I'm a coward. I'm not a monster." Ending 3 — redemption.

**ENDING 4 "Last Song"** (3B → TURN THE MUSIC BACK ON): June runs to the office. Over every speaker, she sings the FreshCo
jingle, off-key, on purpose, as loud as she can. The horde turns and shuffles to the front. You, Walt and Biscuit squeeze
out a ventilation hatch into the cold. From the road you look back: the store lights, June's voice still singing… then it
stops. NARRATOR: "You'll never hear that stupid song the same way again." Ending card, then a final tag: a radio voice,
off-key, singing the jingle from somewhere else. Hook: June is alive?
