# Contributing

Thanks for helping! The most useful contributions:

- **New sets** (`src/sets/`): a hospital, a forest cabin, a spaceship, a school… Same flat style, ink outlines,
  more detail than the characters.
- **New looks** (`src/kit/Stick.tsx`): body colour + one accessory.
- **New SFX** (`scripts/audio/`): synthesized only, no samples or library audio.
- **New stories** (`stories/<story>/` + `src/stories/<story>/`): a full branching script and at least Part 1.
- **TTS integrations**: anything that makes natural voices easier to get.

Before opening a PR:

1. `npm run typecheck` passes.
2. Render a still of anything visual you changed and attach it to the PR.
3. No copyrighted audio, art, or story content from other creators.

Bugs and ideas: open an issue with the composition id, the frame number, and a screenshot.
