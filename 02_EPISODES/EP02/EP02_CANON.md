## 2. Events of EP02 (in order)
1. Vote result: **most of you chose A, THE SILENT FIELD**. Platform B (the Ghost Echo) goes dark; YOU lifts the SF-60 from
   platform A. It is **portable and carried**, not installed. €260,000 − €225,000 = **€35,000**.
2. The 365-day preparation countdown runs out on the control-room console: 365 DAYS REMAINING → **0 · PREPARATION COMPLETE**.
   YOU is inside the bunker; the airlock is sealed (both doors). The surface is still quiet.
3. **Patient Zero:** the first infection is reported in a hospital (isolation ward). An infected patient lies in a bed;
   a doctor leans in to help; the patient's voice (red rings, never audible) infects the doctor. Within hours the
   outbreak has begun; the chain reaction spreads through crowds. (YOU is not present.)
4. **Seven-day collapse:** cities fall silent and dark; the global infection map turns red (clusters on every
   continent; no single origin point is marked); airports close (every departure CANCELLED); hospitals overflow
   (ICU FULL); the emergency ticker ("VOICE-TRANSMITTED PATHOGEN · DO NOT LISTEN TO THE INFECTED · STAY INDOORS") fails.
5. **DAY 7 OF THE OUTBREAK.** In the bunker the lights are still on.
6. **The final government broadcast** reaches the bunker's console and is converted to text only (as always; playback
   disabled). Warnings: **NEVER LISTEN TO UNVERIFIED TRANSMISSIONS.** **NEVER RESPOND TO CHANNEL BLACK-7.** Message:
   "IF YOU ARE RECEIVING THIS MESSAGE, DO NOT RESPOND TO ANY TRANSMISSION ORIGINATING FROM BLACK-7. THE INFECTED CAN
   REPRODUCE FAMILIAR IDENTITIES. DO NOT TRUST THEM." The signal degrades and dies (NO SIGNAL). The warning stays saved
   on the console.
7. Minutes later: **INCOMING TRANSMISSION · CHANNEL: BLACK-7 · SOURCE: UNKNOWN · WARNING: RESTRICTED CHANNEL**.
8. BLACK-7 transcript (text only, sender unknown): **"PLEASE." "I KNOW YOU CAN SEE THIS." "THEY'RE OUTSIDE."**, then
   **"PLEASE ANSWER."**
9. Choice presented (A INVESTIGATE / B DISCONNECT). Cliffhanger: BLACK-7 writes **"WHY AREN'T YOU ANSWERING?"**
   YOU has not responded, not disconnected, and not activated the Silent Field.

## 3. Decisions
- EP01 vote applied: **A — THE SILENT FIELD bought** (budget €35,000). The Ghost Echo was not bought.

  - **A — INVESTIGATE:** ignore the government warning; investigate BLACK-7 and try to establish the source.
  - **B — DISCONNECT:** obey the government; disconnect BLACK-7 and protect the bunker.
  - Neither option costs money. Budget stays €35,000 either way unless EP03 says otherwise.

## 4. Established facts the series must keep
- Everything in EP01 CANON §4, plus:
- The Silent Field is a **portable, hand-carried** device (SF-60 emitter, drawn at `SF_SCALE 0.5` when carried); it is
  **not** a bunker installation and **not** always on. 60 s duration. Unused as of EP02. Whether it is single-use or
  rechargeable is **not established**.
- The bunker console can receive **radio/broadcast transmissions**; like EXT MIC 01 they go through
  SPEECH-TO-TEXT → SCREEN ONLY (speakers off). YOU has never heard a transmission.
- **Channel BLACK-7** is the only named channel. The government prohibited responding to it. The incoming transmission
  came from BLACK-7.
- The government **claims** "the infected can reproduce familiar identities." This is a claim inside the story, not
  confirmed on screen.
- The BLACK-7 sender is **unidentified**: no name, face, voice, gender, contact match or credentials shown. The sender
  appears to know YOU can see (read) the messages; how is **not** explained.
- Outbreak timeline: DAY 0 = end of preparation; Patient Zero in a hospital on day 1; global collapse by DAY 7.
- Consumption rate used on the HUD: 1 day of food and ≈10 L drinking water per day.
